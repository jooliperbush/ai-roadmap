# Miscited market, website and launch design

## Decision

Lead with answer accuracy for B2B SaaS teams; recruit specialist SEO/content agencies as distribution partners. Treat this as a positioning hypothesis pending paid customer evidence. Broad visibility is crowded and action recommendations already exist at Peec, Profound and others. Enterprise governance is a later option, constrained by current deployment maturity.

## Website

Use the existing Fastify public route and audit endpoint. Build a distinctive warm-paper/red-ink editorial identity with a large serif headline, a worked answer/correction exhibit, a concise capability strip, buyer-specific outcomes, a five-step workflow, inspectable measurement principles, an early-access offer, FAQs and an audit form. Preserve original navigation, audit selectors, accessible form behavior and worked-example interactions. No invented testimonials, customer logos, star counts or live findings. Keep the exhibit explicitly simulated; distinguish configured API surfaces from consumer applications. Centralize FAQs so visible content matches structured data. Remove unsupported fixed subscription promises from marketing metadata, labelling internal historical plan prices as planning examples.

## Deliverables

1. Source-linked market analysis, competitor comparison, segment ranking, pricing experiments, opportunity scenarios, risks and customer-discovery protocol.
2. Implemented responsive marketing site, tested at desktop/tablet/mobile and reduced motion.
3. Founder-led 30/60/90-day launch plan, GitHub readiness and organic star acquisition, Product Hunt preparation/day-of/post-launch, amplification calendar, funnel definitions and budgets.
4. Draft launch assets, repository packaging text, demo storyboard and a working channel/funnel tracker.

## Acceptance

Original behavior tests pass; new tests catch misleading partial-provider coverage, contradictory offers and broken conversion. No messages sent, stars purchased, accounts manipulated, repository published/renamed or site deployed as part of preparing this work. Launch assets identify facts needing founder verification. Evidence is dated 2026-09-05; no customer interviews or private revenue data were supplied.
